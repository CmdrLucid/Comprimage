## Data flow

```
Home  ──►  Picker  ──►  Settings sheet  ──►  Progress (CompressionService)  ──►  Results  ──►  Compare
           select        per-run settings      one photo at a time, cancel          summary     before/after
           photos        (Continue)            replace mode: batch delete           per photo
```

- **Home** (`home_screen.dart`) requests gallery permission and opens the picker.
- **Picker** (`picker_screen.dart`) returns the selected assets and the settings
  chosen in the sheet, opened by its Continue button.
- **Progress** (`progress_screen.dart`) calls `CompressionService.run`, which
  returns a list of `CompressionResult`.
- **Results** (`results_screen.dart`) shows the summary. Tapping a compressed
  photo opens **Compare** (`compare_screen.dart`).
